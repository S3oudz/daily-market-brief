### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Closed 14.43 Friday, unchanged over the weekend
  since equities don't trade Saturday/Sunday (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. Latest (Aug 26/27): 32.9% bulls, 22.6% neutral, 44.4% bears; next reading
  Thursday, September 3 (Section 3, Section 10).
- **CME FedWatch** — a tool that converts fed funds futures prices into an implied probability of
  the Fed's next rate move; currently ~56% for a September hike, down from Friday's ~60% peak
  (Section 4).
- **Kalshi** — a regulated U.S. prediction-market exchange where traders bet directly on binary
  event outcomes (e.g., "will the Fed hike in September: yes/no"); currently pricing that same
  question at ~48%, diverging from CME FedWatch (Section 4).
- **"Family fight"** — Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's own characterization of internal Fed policy debate
  over the current rate path, per this weekend's reporting — an acknowledgment of genuine internal
  disagreement rather than a managed consensus (Section 4).
- **TAM (total addressable market)** — the total revenue opportunity a company claims it could
  theoretically capture; a company-supplied estimate, not an audited figure. Anthropic is
  reportedly pitching IPO investors a $30 trillion TAM (Section 5) — a number worth treating with
  more skepticism than a disclosed financial statement.
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO, containing audited
  financials and legally binding disclosures. Anthropic has not confirmed filing one as of this
