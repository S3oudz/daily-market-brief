## 10. Connect the Dots *(most speculative — facts flagged, inferences flagged)*

still-active Hormuz blockade. **Common-sense logic:** these two moves are pulling in opposite
directions from a macro standpoint — equities are pricing "inflation is cooling," while oil is

(medium confidence):** this divergence can't persist indefinitely. Either oil's climb stalls (the
Hormuz risk gets priced in and settles, consistent with Trump's retreat from the toll proposal
Tuesday), or oil keeps climbing and starts showing up in the next few CPI prints — directly
undercutting the disinflation narrative that drove Tuesday's rally in the first place. Warsh's own
caution ("that is not my view" on mission-accomplished) may partly reflect this exact tension: a
Fed chair aware that today's good print could be undone by an energy shock already visible in the
oil market as he spoke.

**Second thread — fact:** IBM's crash was explicitly attributed to enterprise clients redirecting
software/consulting budgets toward AI hardware (servers, storage, memory) ahead of expected price
increases. **Fact:** Micron (MU +4.69%) and other memory/hardware names rallied the same day.
**Common-sense logic:** if IBM's own commentary is accurate, this isn't just "IBM had a bad
quarter" — it's evidence of a broader budget reallocation happening across enterprise IT right
now, with hardware winning and software/services losing share of wallet. **Inference (medium-low
confidence):** watch upcoming earnings from other enterprise-software-heavy names (ServiceNow,

### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; expected S&P volatility. Low (<16) = calm; high (>25) = stressed.
- **AAII survey** — weekly poll of retail investors on whether they're bullish/bearish for the next 6 months.
- **CPI (Consumer Price Index)** — measures what consumers pay for goods/services; the headline inflation number markets watch most closely.
- **PPI (Producer Price Index)** — measures what producers/wholesalers pay before goods reach consumers; often a leading indicator for future CPI.
- **Strait of Hormuz** — the narrow shipping channel between Iran and Oman through which roughly 20% of the world's oil and gas trade passes; a blockade directly threatens global energy supply.
- **ETF proxy** — an exchange-traded fund used to represent an index or asset (SPY for the S&P, GLD for gold); note ETF proxies only trade during US market hours, unlike crypto.
- **Basis point (bp/pp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point; used to measure small spread changes precisely.
- **Sympathy move** — when a stock moves (usually down) because a related company in the same sector reported bad news, even without its own new information.
