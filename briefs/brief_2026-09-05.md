### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. 14.53 this morning, +1.47% — a small uptick after
  three straight declines (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. Latest confirmed reading (week ending Sept 2, unchanged since Thursday):
  39.7% bulls, 22.7% neutral, 37.6% bears; next print due Thursday, September 10 (Section 3).
- **CME FedWatch** — a tool that converts fed funds futures prices into an implied probability of
  the Fed's next rate move; at 52.6% for a September hike as of Friday, net down from Wednesday's
  54.6% despite a hot jobs report (Section 4, Section 9.5).
- **Kalshi** — a regulated U.S. prediction-market exchange where traders bet directly on binary
  event outcomes; pricing the September hike at ~59%, a gap from CME that widened this week
  (Section 1, Section 10).
- **Nonfarm payrolls (NFP)** — the Bureau of Labor Statistics' monthly count of jobs added or lost
  across the U.S. economy, excluding farm workers; August's reading (+162,000) beat consensus by
  nearly 3x (Section 4).
- **CPI / PPI** — two separate inflation gauges due next week (PPI Sept 10, CPI Sept 11) — the last
  major data before the September 16 FOMC decision, landing during the Fed's pre-meeting "quiet
  period" (Section 9.5, Section 11).
- **FOMC quiet period** — the informal, several-week window before each Federal Reserve meeting
  during which officials avoid public commentary on monetary policy; begins today (Sept 5) and
  runs through the September 16 decision (Section 9.5, Section 11).
- **10-year Treasury yield** — the interest rate the U.S. government pays to borrow for 10 years; a
  benchmark for mortgage rates, corporate borrowing costs, and gold's relative attractiveness as a
  non-yielding asset. Held near ~4.76% Friday, essentially flat (Section 1, Section 7).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO, containing audited
  financials and legally binding disclosures. Anthropic confidentially filed one June 1, 2026; a
  public version is reportedly possible after September 7 (Section 10 Thread Five).
- **SMH** — the main semiconductor ETF and standard benchmark for chip-sector performance. Closed
  +2.59% Friday, the best-performing ETF in this brief's basket (Section 9).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, so the next day's
  Scorecard has something objective to grade.
