multi-sourced via search); **PREDICTION/my read** that treating Monday's futures bump as evidence
the Iran situation is resolving peacefully reads more certainty into one headline than the full
reporting supports.

💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** the bullish framing and the military-buildup framing are both
accurate reports of real events — neither is fabricated or wrong. **Inference (medium confidence):**

### Glossary
- **Basis point (bp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point.
- **FOMC** — the Federal Open Market Committee, the Fed body that sets interest-rate policy.
  September meeting minutes due Wednesday, October 7 (Sections 10, 11).
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the
  next 30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25–30 = genuine stress. Currently 15.31, still Friday's
  close (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment;
  the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric. Unchanged at −11.9;
  next reading Thursday, October 8 (Section 1).
- **WTI vs. Brent crude** — two different crude oil benchmarks; WTI is the US domestic benchmark,
  Brent is the international (North Sea) benchmark and is more directly exposed to Middle East
  supply disruptions (Section 7).
- **IEA** — the International Energy Agency, an intergovernmental body that coordinated the G7's
  100-million-barrel reserve release announced Friday (Sections 1, 7).
- **Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR)** — the US government's own emergency oil stockpile,
  separate from the coordinated G7/IEA release; currently at 283.8 million barrels, a 44-year low
  (Section 7).
- **8-K** — a filing public companies must make with the SEC within days of a material corporate
