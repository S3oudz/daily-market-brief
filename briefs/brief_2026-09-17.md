verifiable stories that only look connected because both are "oil." A reader tracking only Brent

💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** the API and EIA inventory reports diverge this week (7.14M build vs.
0.64M draw), which is itself worth watching for revision risk. **Inference (medium confidence):**
if the EIA's more authoritative figure trends toward confirming a build in coming weeks, that's a

➡️ *What you could do:* Watch next week's EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report for whether it

### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the
  next 30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Sat at 17.71 Wednesday, up modestly on the
  Fed decision (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. This week's reading was still pending as of this brief (Section 3).
- **FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee)** — the Fed's rate-setting body; 12 voting members.
  Wednesday's decision was a unanimous 12-0 vote to hike 25bp (Section 1, 4).
- **Dot plot / Summary of Economic Projections (SEP)** — each FOMC member's anonymous projection
  for future rates, published quarterly; September's median points to one more 25bp hike this
  year (Section 1, 4, 10).
- **API vs. EIA crude inventories** — two separate weekly US oil-inventory reports: the American
  Petroleum Institute's (industry-funded, released first, less authoritative) and the Energy
  Information Administration's (US government, released a day later, the official number). They
  diverged sharply this week — a 7.14M-barrel build (API) versus a 0.64M-barrel draw (EIA)
  (Section 7, 9.5).
- **Brent vs. WTI** — the two main global oil benchmarks; Brent prices most non-US crude and is
  more sensitive to Middle East/global supply risk, while WTI is the US domestic benchmark and
  more sensitive to US-specific inventory and production data (Section 7, 9.5).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO. Anthropic
