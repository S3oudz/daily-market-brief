**Second thread — fact:** Friday's session showed sharp dispersion within AI-linked names (NVDA

### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; expected S&P volatility. Low (<16) = calm; high (>25) = stressed.
- **AAII survey** — weekly poll of retail investors on whether they're bullish/bearish for the next 6 months.
- **When-issued trading** — a provisional trading period (e.g. "SKHYV") before a security's official listing (e.g. "SKHY"), used to establish a market price ahead of full trading.
- **IRGC** — Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the branch of Iran's military responsible for the Strait of Hormuz attacks and closure declaration.
- **Strait of Hormuz** — the narrow shipping channel between Iran and Oman through which roughly 20% of the world's oil and gas trade passes; a closure directly threatens global energy supply.
- **CENTCOM** — US Central Command, the US military command responsible for the Middle East, source of the strike confirmations cited here.
- **ETF proxy** — an exchange-traded fund used to represent an index or asset (SPY for the S&P, GLD for gold); note ETF proxies only trade during US market hours, unlike crypto.
- **ADR (American Depositary Receipt)** — a US-listed security representing shares of a foreign company (e.g. SK Hynix's SKHY), allowing US investors to trade it without a foreign brokerage account.
- **Basis point (bp/pp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point; used to measure small spread changes precisely.
