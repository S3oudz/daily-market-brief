### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; expected S&P volatility. Low (<16) = calm; high (>25) = stressed.
- **AAII survey** — weekly poll of retail investors on whether they're bullish/bearish for the next 6 months.
- **When-issued trading** — a provisional trading period (e.g. "SKHYV") before a security's official listing (e.g. "SKHY"), used to establish a market price ahead of full trading.
- **ADR / ADS** — American Depositary Receipt/Share; how a foreign company's shares trade on a US exchange (e.g. SK Hynix's SKHY).
- **Lock-up expiration** — the date after an IPO when early investors/insiders are first allowed to sell shares, often creating selling pressure.
- **Hyperscaler** — a company operating massive-scale cloud computing infrastructure (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud; Meta is positioning to join this group).
- **ETF proxy** — an exchange-traded fund used to represent an index or asset (SPY for the S&P, GLD for gold).
- **Basis point (bp/pp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point; used to measure small spread changes precisely.
