💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** three independent pressures — a growing federal deficit, weakening
demand at Treasury auctions, and a war with a rising, CBO-quantified monthly cost — are landing
on the bond market at the same time as elevated oil prices (Section 7) and a Fed decision that's
a coin flip. **Inference (medium confidence):** when yields rise because investors want more
compensation to hold government debt (a supply/demand-for-Treasuries story), that's a different
and arguably more durable problem than "yields rose because the Fed might hike" — a rate decision
resolves in an afternoon; a widening deficit and rising war costs don't resolve at 2:00pm ET
today.

➡️ *What you could do:* Don't treat today's FOMC decision as the thing that "fixes" high yields
even if the Fed holds. Watch the *next* 10-year and 30-year Treasury auction results (calendar
varies; check TreasuryDirect) for whether bid-to-cover ratios keep deteriorating — that's the

### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the
  next 30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Sat at 17.20 Tuesday, essentially flat
  (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. This week's reading (38.0% bulls / 39.3% bears) won't update again until
  Thursday, September 17 (Section 3).
- **FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee)** — the Fed's rate-setting body; 12 voting members. The
  decision and press conference both land this afternoon, Wednesday, September 16, delivered by
  Chair Kevin Warsh (Section 1, 10, 11).
- **Bid-to-cover ratio** — at a Treasury auction, the dollar amount of bids received divided by
  the dollar amount of debt sold; a lower ratio (like this month's 2.39) means weaker investor
  demand, forcing primary dealers to absorb more of the issuance themselves (Section 4, 9.5).
- **East-West Pipeline** — Saudi Arabia's main crude pipeline (7 million b/d capacity) that
  bypasses the Strait of Hormuz entirely; shut by drone strikes September 10-11, confirmed back
  at full capacity as of this brief (Section 1, 7).
- **CBO (Congressional Budget Office)** — Congress's own nonpartisan scorekeeper for the federal
  budget; its estimate that the Iran war has cost $38 billion and is rising $3 billion a month is
  a government, not media, number (Section 4, 9.5).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO. Anthropic
  confidentially filed one June 1, 2026; reporting still points to a public filing by late
  September or October, unfiled as of this morning's check (Section 10, 11).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, then grade it
  honestly once data exists (Section 10).
