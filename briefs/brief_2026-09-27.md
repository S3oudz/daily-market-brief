headline built around "record verdict." **Label: FACT** on the verdict amount and the same-day
stock move; **PREDICTION/my read** that the market's reaction, not the verdict's size, is the
better guide to whether this matters going forward.

💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** two things can both be true — a verdict can be historic in legal
terms and immaterial in market terms — and headlines optimized for "biggest ever" naturally
undersell the second half.

➡️ *What you could do:* When a "record" legal verdict headline crosses for a mega-cap company,

### Glossary
- **Basis point (bp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point.
- **FOMC** — the Federal Open Market Committee, the Fed body that sets interest-rate policy.
  Next meeting October 27-28 (Section 10).
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures) price index** — the Fed's preferred inflation
  gauge, released monthly by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. August data lands Wednesday,
  September 30, 8:30am ET (Section 11).
- **ISM Manufacturing PMI** — a monthly survey-based reading of US factory activity; above 50
  = expansion, below 50 = contraction. Due Thursday, October 1 (Section 11).
- **JOLTS** — the Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey, a monthly Bureau of Labor Statistics
  read on how many open jobs employers report; due Tuesday, September 29 (Section 11).
- **Strait of Hormuz** — the narrow shipping channel between Iran and Oman through which
  roughly a fifth of global oil trade passes; the subject of Iran's rejected reopening
  proposal (Section 4).
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the
  next 30 days. Below 16 = calm. Still 14.87, Friday's close (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish
  sentiment; the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric.
  Unchanged at −15.4; next update Thursday, October 1 (Section 3).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company files before an IPO; can be filed
  confidentially first before a public version appears on EDGAR. Anthropic's remains
  confidential (Section 10).
- **Special verdict** — a jury verdict that answers specific factual questions posed by the
  court, rather than a single lump verdict; a judge then applies those factual findings to
  determine damages — the format of the New Mexico jury's Friday finding against Meta
  (Section 1).
