### Glossary
- **Basis point (bp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point. The Fed's September 16 hike of 25bp moved its target rate from 3.50%-3.75% to 3.75%-4.00% (Section 10).
- **FOMC** — the Federal Open Market Committee, the Fed body that sets interest-rate policy; meets roughly every six weeks. Next meeting Oct 27-28 (Section 10).
- **Flash PMI / Composite PMI Output Index** — an early, survey-based reading of business activity across manufacturing and services; above 50 = expansion, below 50 = contraction. September's flash reading of 58.4 was the highest since July 2021 (Section 4).
- **Rule 144 filing** — an SEC notice that lets a company insider sell restricted or control stock; often filed ahead of a lockup expiration and can pressure a stock even without new operating news (Section 8).
- **Lockup expiration** — the date after an IPO when early investors/insiders are first allowed to sell shares; can create selling pressure independent of company fundamentals (Section 8).
- **Consensus analyst price target** — the average 12-month price target across analysts covering a stock; when a stock closes back below a target it recently cleared, it signals the market isn't yet validating a further target raise (Section 1).
- **Strait of Hormuz** — the narrow shipping channel between Iran and Oman through which roughly a fifth of global oil trade passes; disruption risk there is a standing driver of the oil risk premium this brief tracks (Sections 4, 7, 9.5).
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next 30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment, published Thursdays; the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric. Frozen at −24.5 as of this brief; update due today (Section 3).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company files before an IPO; can be filed confidentially first before a public version appears on EDGAR. Anthropic's remains confidential; press reporting (not a filing) now points to a ~$2T+ valuation (Section 10).
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures) price index** — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, released monthly by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. Next release Wednesday, Sep 30, for August data (Section 11).
