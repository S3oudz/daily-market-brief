### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. 15.20 this morning, down 6.98% — a sharp reversal
  of Tuesday's spike (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. Latest *confirmed* reading (week ending Aug 26): 32.9% bulls, 22.6% neutral,
  44.4% bears; this week's update was not yet visible on AAII's own site at pull time (Section 3,
  Section 10).
- **Beige Book** — the Fed's periodic, anecdotal survey of regional economic conditions gathered
  from business contacts across its twelve districts, published roughly two weeks before each FOMC
  meeting. September's edition (released Sept 2) named energy costs as the primary inflation driver
  while describing employment growth as "very slight" (Section 1, Section 4).
- **CME FedWatch** — a tool that converts fed funds futures prices into an implied probability of
  the Fed's next rate move; most recently reported near ~66% for a September hike (Section 4,
  Section 10).
- **Kalshi** — a regulated U.S. prediction-market exchange where traders bet directly on binary
  event outcomes; last reported near 59% on the same September-hike question, no fresher reading
  found today (Section 10).
- **JOLTs** — the Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey, a Bureau of Labor Statistics report on the
  number of open positions and the pace of hires/quits; today's release covers July data (Section
  11).
- **10-year Treasury yield** — the interest rate the U.S. government pays to borrow for 10 years; a
  benchmark for mortgage rates, corporate borrowing costs, and gold's relative attractiveness as a
  non-yielding asset. Hit 4.818% intraday Wednesday before easing (Section 4, Section 7).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO, containing audited
  financials and legally binding disclosures. Anthropic filed one confidentially in June; no public
  version exists yet (Section 10 Thread Five).
- **SMH** — the main semiconductor ETF and standard benchmark for chip-sector performance. Closed
  +0.22% Wednesday, underperforming both the broad market and small caps (Section 9).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, so the next day's
  Scorecard has something objective to grade.
