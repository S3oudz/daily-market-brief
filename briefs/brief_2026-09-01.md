### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. 14.92 this morning, up 3.4% overnight but still
  calm (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. Latest (week ending Aug 26): 32.9% bulls, 22.6% neutral, 44.4% bears; next
  reading Thursday, September 3 (Section 3, Section 10).
- **CME FedWatch** — a tool that converts fed funds futures prices into an implied probability of
  the Fed's next rate move; now ~66% for a September hike, up sharply since Warsh's Jackson Hole
  speech (Section 4).
- **Kalshi** — a regulated U.S. prediction-market exchange where traders bet directly on binary
  event outcomes; currently pricing the same September-hike question at ~47%, a widening
  divergence from CME FedWatch (Section 4).
- **ISM Manufacturing PMI** — a monthly survey-based gauge of U.S. factory activity; above 50 =
  expansion. July's reading was 55.6, the strongest since May 2022; August's reading is due 10am
  ET today (Section 11).
- **Rule 135** — an SEC provision letting a company publicly confirm it has confidentially filed
  for an IPO without disclosing financial details; the basis of Anthropic's boilerplate June 1
  announcement (Section 9.5).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO, containing audited
  financials and legally binding disclosures. Anthropic filed one confidentially in June; no
  public version exists yet (Section 5, Section 9.5).
- **Revenue-recognition timing** — when a company can count revenue as "earned" under accounting
  rules, distinct from when a deal is signed or its total size is known; the core of the Marvell/
  Broadcom test (Section 5, Section 10 Thread One).
- **SMH** — the main semiconductor ETF and standard benchmark for chip-sector performance. Closed
  +0.64% Monday, diverging positive from the broad index (Section 9).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, so the next day's
  Scorecard has something objective to grade.
