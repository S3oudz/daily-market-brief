CNBC's "Washington scrambles to meet calls for AI guardrails while the window to act closes."

**Fact — an Anthropic researcher, Jacob Coxon, resigned this week**, stating neither Anthropic nor
OpenAI is "acting responsibly" and that they are "racing straight to self-improving

💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** three competing frontier-AI CEOs used the same weekend to publicly

💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** there is a real, checkable distance between "CEOs call for pacing
model capability" and "CEOs are cutting AI infrastructure spending" — the former happened this

### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the
  next 30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Frozen at Friday's 15.84 (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. This week's reading (38.0% bulls / 39.3% bears) won't update again until
  Thursday, September 17 (Section 3).
- **FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee)** — the Fed's rate-setting body; meets September 15-16,
  with the decision and press conference both Wednesday afternoon, delivered by Chair Kevin Warsh
  (Section 1, Section 11).
- **Brent vs. WTI** — Brent is the global oil benchmark (more exposed to Middle East/Strait of
  Hormuz risk); WTI is the US domestic benchmark. Brent jumped to $102.79 overnight on the Saudi
  pipeline shutdown (Section 1, Section 7).
- **East-West Pipeline** — Saudi Arabia's main crude pipeline that bypasses the Strait of Hormuz
  entirely, running from the Eastern Province to the Red Sea; its precautionary shutdown this
  weekend removes a key alternative to the strait, which is why it moved oil prices sharply
  (Section 7).
- **"Pacing the frontier"** — the phrase from Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's September 2026 essay
  calling for slower AI *capability* improvement (not a halt to spending or training) to let
  safety/alignment work catch up (Section 4, Section 9.5).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO. Anthropic
  confidentially filed one June 1, 2026; reporting now points to a public filing by end of
  September, still unfiled as of this morning's direct EDGAR check (Section 5).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, then grade it
  honestly once data exists (Section 10).
