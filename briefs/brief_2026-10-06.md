### Glossary
- **Bear steepener** — a yield-curve move where long-term rates rise faster than (or while)
  short-term rates fall, widening the gap between them; typically reflects near-term rate-cut
  expectations alongside longer-run inflation or fiscal-risk pricing (Sections 3, 4).
- **ISM Services PMI** — a monthly survey-based gauge of US services-sector activity; above 50 =
  expansion, below 50 = contraction. September read: 54.9% (Section 1).
- **FOMC** — the Federal Open Market Committee, the Fed body that sets interest-rate policy.
  September meeting minutes due Wednesday, October 7 (Sections 10, 11).
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25–30 = genuine stress. Currently 15.52 (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment;
  the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric. Unchanged at
  −11.8/−11.9; next reading Thursday, October 8 (Section 1).
- **2s/10s spread** — the gap between 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields; a classic recession/rate-
  expectation indicator, relevant here because the two moved in opposite directions Monday
  (Section 3).
- **IEA** — the International Energy Agency, the intergovernmental body that coordinated the G7's
  oil-reserve release referenced in Section 7.
- **Equity value vs. enterprise value** — equity value is what shareholders receive ($80.9B for
  WBD); enterprise value adds net debt assumed in the deal — the likely source of the higher
  "~$110B" figure in earlier briefs (Section 5).
