### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the
  next 30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Fell to 14.81 Friday, the calmest reading in
  this brief's recent run (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays; the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric.
  Frozen at −24.5 since the week ending Sep 16; next update Sep 24 (Section 3).
- **Chairman emeritus** — an honorary, non-executive title given to a former chairman who steps
  back from formal board leadership but typically retains a board seat and an advisory role, as
  Warren Buffett now holds at Berkshire Hathaway (Section 5).
- **Socket share** — in chip manufacturing, the percentage of a device's components (e.g., an
  iPhone's modem) supplied by a given chipmaker; Qualcomm's falling iPhone socket share as Apple
  shifts to in-house modems drove Friday's decline (Section 8).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO. Anthropic
  confidentially filed one June 1, 2026; reporting continues to point to an October public listing,
  unfiled as of this morning's check (Section 10, 11).
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures) price index** — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge,
  released monthly by the Bureau of Economic Analysis; generally considered more important to Fed
  policy than CPI. Next release confirmed for Sep 30 (Section 11).
- **Contrarian indicator** — a signal that's historically more informative when read in reverse
  (e.g., extreme retail bearishness has often preceded further market strength, not weakness) —
  this week's frozen AAII reading is being tracked as a live test of that pattern (Section 3, 10).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, then grade it
  honestly once data exists (Section 10).
