### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Ticked up to 15.30 overnight, still calm but the
  first increase all week (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. Latest confirmed reading (week ending Sept 2): 39.7% bulls, 22.7% neutral,
  37.6% bears; next print due Thursday, September 10 (Section 3).
- **CME FedWatch** — a tool that converts fed funds futures prices into an implied probability of
  the Fed's next rate move; source dispersion widened today (54-68% range across trackers for a
  hike), itself a signal of market indecision heading into this week's inflation prints (Section 10).
- **Kalshi** — a regulated U.S. prediction-market exchange where traders bet directly on binary
  event outcomes; showing wide dispersion (54-68%) on a September hike as of this morning, with
  cut odds down near 1% (Section 10).
- **Strait of Hormuz** — the narrow waterway between Iran and Oman through which a large share of
  the world's seaborne oil trade passes; site of the existing US naval blockade and Iran's
  still-undeclared exclusion zone (Section 4).
- **Joint War Committee (Lloyd's Market Association)** — the body that designates high-risk marine
  war zones for insurance purposes; has already expanded its Persian Gulf "high-risk" zone ahead of
  Iran's specific exclusion-zone declaration (Section 4, Section 10 Thread Seven).
- **FOMC quiet period** — the informal, several-week window before each Federal Reserve meeting
  during which officials avoid public commentary on monetary policy; runs through the September 16
  decision (Section 11).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO, containing audited
  financials and legally binding disclosures. Anthropic confidentially filed one June 1, 2026; a
  direct EDGAR search confirmed for a third straight day that no public version exists, even as
  reported target figures ($1.75-1.8T valuation, up to $75B raised) got more specific (Section 5).
- **SMH** — the main semiconductor ETF and standard benchmark for chip-sector performance. Closed
  +2.59% Friday, the best-performing ETF in this brief's basket for the week, now four days stale
  (Section 9).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, then grade it honestly
  once data exists — as done today with Brent clearing its Monday-set level (Section 1, Section 10
  Thread Six).
