hub) and the M/T Stark 1 near Jask — in response to Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps attacks on
US Navy-linked vessels. Iran said it retaliated against US-linked shipping.
([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/05/nx-s1-5959159/us-iran-warships-targeted),
[Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609058771))

facility — an underground complex near the Natanz enrichment site, built beneath hundreds of
meters of rock — "very soon." Al Jazeera's own coverage frames this as a threat that has
recurred before without yet resulting in a strike, which is worth tracking rather than treating
each restatement as new information.
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/5/why-is-trump-threatening-to-strike-irans-pickaxe-mountain-again-now))

**Fact — the price impact already visible before this weekend's escalation.** Labor Day weekend
gas prices hit $4.14/gallon nationally, the highest on record for the holiday, surpassing the
prior 2012 record; diesel hit roughly $5.82-5.85/gallon, also a record, beating the June 2022 high.
GasBuddy and CNBC both attribute this to Strait of Hormuz shipping disruption plus drone strikes on
Russian refineries cutting global refined-fuel supply — not solely this weekend's news.

[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/05/americans-hit-with-record-high-labor-day-weekend-gasoline-prices.html))

💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** this is not a new conflict — it's an escalation within a US-Iran
war that has been running for months (the data bundle's own headline feed independently surfaced

### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Frozen at Friday's 14.53 over the weekend
  (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. Latest confirmed reading (week ending Sept 2): 39.7% bulls, 22.7% neutral,
  37.6% bears; next print due Thursday, September 10 (Section 3).
- **CME FedWatch** — a tool that converts fed funds futures prices into an implied probability of
  the Fed's next rate move; frozen at Friday's 52.6% for a September hike over the weekend
  (Section 1).
- **Kalshi** — a regulated U.S. prediction-market exchange where traders bet directly on binary
