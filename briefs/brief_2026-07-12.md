**Iran declared the Strait of Hormuz closed, and the US struck back a third time.** Iran's IRGC
Navy announced early Sunday local time that the strait — the route for roughly 20% of the

an "unauthorized route." The attack set the ship on fire; one crew member is missing.

7:15pm ET, per CENTCOM, targeting air-surveillance radars, missile/drone storage and launch
sites, and surface-to-air missile launchers — strikes described as "ongoing" at the time of
reporting. ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/us-iran-war-trump-ceasefire-talks-strait-of-hormuz/)) Separately, Oman-mediated talks between Iran's foreign minister and Oman's foreign
minister in Muscat wrapped Saturday with, per Al Jazeera, "little decided."

💡 *What it means:* **Fact:** this is the first *formal closure declaration* of this specific

undercut with shipping attacks. **Inference (medium confidence):** a formal closure declaration
is a materially different signal than another tanker attack, because it removes the ambiguity

disrupted") — Monday's open is the first chance to see whether that ambiguity-driven calm

### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; expected S&P volatility. Low (<16) = calm; high (>25) = stressed.
- **AAII survey** — weekly poll of retail investors on whether they're bullish/bearish for the next 6 months.
- **When-issued trading** — a provisional trading period (e.g. "SKHYV") before a security's official listing (e.g. "SKHY"), used to establish a market price ahead of full trading.
- **IRGC** — Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the branch of Iran's military responsible for the Strait of Hormuz attacks and closure declaration.
- **Strait of Hormuz** — the narrow shipping channel between Iran and Oman through which roughly 20% of the world's oil and gas trade passes; a closure directly threatens global energy supply.
- **CENTCOM** — US Central Command, the US military command responsible for the Middle East, source of the strike confirmations cited here.
- **MOU (memorandum of understanding)** — the June 17, 2026 US-Iran agreement intended to formally end the broader conflict within 60 days; repeatedly undercut since by Iranian attacks on Hormuz shipping.
- **ETF proxy** — an exchange-traded fund used to represent an index or asset (SPY for the S&P, GLD for gold); note ETF proxies only trade during US market hours, unlike crypto.
- **Basis point (bp/pp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point; used to measure small spread changes precisely.
