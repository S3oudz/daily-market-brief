### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the
  next 30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. Fell to 15.44 Thursday, a sharp drop
  consistent with a calm, broad rally (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays; the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric.
  This week's spread was −24.5, among the most bearish readings this cycle (Section 3).
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures) price index** — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge,
