### Glossary
- **Basis point (bp)** — 1/100th of a percentage point. The 10-year yield's Friday move was less than 1bp, even though the level itself is historically extreme (Section 1).
- **FOMC** — the Federal Open Market Committee, the Fed body that sets interest-rate policy. Next meeting Oct 27-28 (Section 10).
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures) price index** — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, released monthly by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. August data lands Wednesday, September 30, 8:30am ET (Section 11).
- **ISM Manufacturing PMI** — a monthly survey-based reading of US factory activity; above 50 = expansion, below 50 = contraction. Due Thursday, October 1 (Section 11).
- **10-year Treasury yield** — the return the US government pays to borrow for 10 years; a benchmark "risk-free" rate that mortgages and corporate debt are priced off of. Closed at a fresh high of 5.163% Friday (Section 1, 4).
- **Strait of Hormuz** — the narrow shipping channel between Iran and Oman through which roughly a fifth of global oil trade passes; US-Iran talks over reopening it drove Friday's oil pullback (Sections 4, 7).
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next 30 days. Below 16 = calm. Fell to 14.87 Friday (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment; the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric. Unchanged at −15.4 this week; next update Thursday, October 1 (Section 3).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company files before an IPO; can be filed confidentially first before a public version appears on EDGAR. Anthropic's remains confidential; press reporting now points to a public filing by month-end (Section 10).
- **Concurrent private placement** — when a strategic investor (here, AbbVie) buys shares directly from a company at the same price as its public IPO, alongside the public offering (Section 5).
