### Glossary
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 25 = stressed. 14.32 this morning, down 5.79% — a third straight
  daily decline (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment,
  published Thursdays. Latest confirmed reading (week ending Sept 2): 39.7% bulls, 22.7% neutral,
  37.6% bears — bulls overtaking bears for the first time this brief has tracked (Section 3).
- **CME FedWatch** — a tool that converts fed funds futures prices into an implied probability of
  the Fed's next rate move; fell from ~66% to ~54.6% after Wednesday's Waller comments (Section 4,
  Section 10).
- **Kalshi** — a regulated U.S. prediction-market exchange where traders bet directly on binary
  event outcomes; currently pricing the September hike at ~59%, a gap from CME's 54.6% that opened
  this week (Section 1, Section 10).
- **Nonfarm payrolls (NFP)** — the Bureau of Labor Statistics' monthly count of jobs added or lost
  across the U.S. economy, excluding farm workers; today's August reading is the last major labor
  data point before the September 16 FOMC meeting (Section 11).
- **PCE / CPI / PPI** — three separate inflation gauges the Fed tracks; PCE is the Fed's preferred
  measure, while CPI (Sept 11) and PPI (Sept 10) are the two specific prints Waller said would
  determine his September vote (Section 4, Section 11).
- **10-year Treasury yield** — the interest rate the U.S. government pays to borrow for 10 years; a
  benchmark for mortgage rates, corporate borrowing costs, and gold's relative attractiveness as a
  non-yielding asset. Eased to ~4.77% Thursday after touching 4.818% Wednesday (Section 4, Section 7).
- **S-1** — the SEC registration statement a company must file before an IPO, containing audited
  financials and legally binding disclosures. Anthropic is reportedly preparing to file one in the
  "coming weeks" (Section 10 Thread Five).
- **SMH** — the main semiconductor ETF and standard benchmark for chip-sector performance. Closed
  +0.39% Thursday, lagging the broad market despite Nvidia's solid day (Section 9).
- **Falsifiable version** — this brief's standing discipline for speculative threads: state in
  advance what specific, checkable outcome would confirm or refute the read, so the next day's
  Scorecard has something objective to grade.
