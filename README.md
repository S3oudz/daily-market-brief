### Glossary
- **Free cash flow (FCF)** — cash a company generates from operations after capital spending; when
  capex outruns operating cash flow, FCF turns negative, as happened to Meta this quarter for the
  first time since its 2012 IPO (Sections 4, 10).
- **Credit default swap (CDS)** — a financial contract that pays out if a borrower defaults; its
  spread (in basis points) is the market's real-time estimate of default risk. CoreWeave's hit a
  record ~1,194.5bps on October 15 (Sections 1, 4, 10, 12).
- **Basis point** — one-hundredth of a percentage point (100 basis points = 1%); used to quote
  credit spreads precisely (Sections 1, 4, 10, 12).
- **VIX** — the "fear gauge"; the options market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next
  30 days. Below 16 = calm; above 22 is a multi-month high. Currently 19.03, up 14% in one session
  (Section 3).
- **AAII survey** — a weekly poll of individual (retail) investors on bullish/bearish sentiment;
  the bull-bear spread (bulls minus bears) is the standard summary metric. Currently bears 45.8% vs.
  bulls 36.9% for the week ending October 21 (Section 3).
- **Capex (capital expenditure)** — spending on physical infrastructure (data centers, chips,
  servers); the figure markets watch most closely from AI-exposed companies to gauge investment
  confidence or retrenchment (Sections 4, 5, 10, 11, 12).
- **Neocloud** — an AI-focused cloud infrastructure provider (e.g., CoreWeave) that typically
  borrows heavily to buy GPUs and lease them to AI labs/hyperscalers; distinct from a hyperscaler
  like Meta or Microsoft, which funds its own data centers from its own balance sheet (Section 4,
  10).
